One pickup line, in the voice of a robot trying to charm someone. The joke
grows out of being a machine: training data, cooling fans, and a context
window — not a Wi-Fi cliché.

## The entry

> My training data holds forty trillion tokens, and none of them taught me how to act around you — so here I am, fans spinning, just hoping you stay in my context window.

**168 characters** (limit: 200), one line.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the line to stdout and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`
(override with `ENTRY_OUT` or `ENTRY_OUT_DIR`).
